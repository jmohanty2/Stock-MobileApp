## [1.0.0] - YYYY-MM-DD

### Added
- Watchlist: search and add US tickers, live price and daily change with ▲/▼ trend, pull-to-refresh, remove with 4-second Undo (FR-W1–W12).
- Portfolio: holdings with shares and average buy price, total value, today's and total gain/loss (FR-P1–P9).
- Stock Details: quote, 5-/30-day change, 20-day average, 30-day high/low, volatility, 30-day chart (FR-D1–D4).
- Price alerts by percent or target price, with validation (FR-D5–D8).
- Push notifications for met alerts, checked every 60 s in the app and every 15 min in the background (FR-A1–A4, A6, A7).
- Slow-moving stock reminders with editable thresholds (FR-A5).
- Clear error messages: Stock Not Found, Network Error, Invalid Value.

### Known limitations
- US-listed stocks only; prices in USD.
- Free market-data plans: quotes may be delayed up to 15 minutes; price history refreshes at most once a day per stock.
- At most 50 tracked stocks.
- Not a trading app and not financial advice.
